The tally is where the money leaks

Listahan sa karton

Parts get written on cardboard, or not at all. By closing time nobody remembers who took the spark plug or which motor it went into.

Compute ulit

The total gets added on a calculator twice and still comes out different. Labor is guessed, discounts are forgotten.

Bakit ganito kamahal?

The customer sees one number with no breakdown, so every bill turns into a debate at the counter.

Talyer Tally keeps the tally from the moment the motor rolls in to the moment it is paid.

How it works

From drop-off to bayad in four steps

Scroll through one real job: a Honda Click with a noisy CVT.

  1. 01

    Log the motor

    Type the customer's name and pick the motorcycle. Plate, phone and the problem are optional, so the job starts in seconds.

    Name + motorcycle onlyAssign a mechanic
  2. 02

    Take parts off the shelf

    The mechanic taps each part as he pulls it. Stock goes down by itself, and the part lands on that motor's job order.

    Stock updates itselfLow-stock alerts
  3. 03

    Watch the tally build

    Every line shows the part, who took it and when. Add labor from presets like Change oil ₱100 or CVT cleaning ₱300.

    Who took it, and whenLabor presets
  4. 04

    Bill out and give sukli

    The owner sees one clear total, picks Cash, GCash, Maya or Card, and the app works out the change. The receipt is ready to copy.

FAQ

Mga tanong

Is the demo free?

Yes. The demo runs in your browser with a sample shop already loaded. There is nothing to sign up for.

Where is my data saved?

In this version, everything stays on the device you use. Sharing jobs between the owner's phone and each mechanic's phone is the next thing being built.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Open it once while online, add it to your home screen, and it keeps working when the signal drops.

Can I use my own parts list and prices?

Yes. The owner can add, edit and remove parts, set the selling price and cost, and restock in one tap. The sample parts are only there to show how it works.

Can mechanics see how much the shop earns?

No. Mechanics only see their own jobs and the selling price of parts. Costs, profit and reports are for the owner.

Is there an Android app?

You can install the demo on Android today from Chrome. A native Android app built with Flutter is in the works.
